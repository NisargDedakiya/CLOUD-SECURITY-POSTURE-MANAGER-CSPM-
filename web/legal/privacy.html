1. Who we are

Aegis ("we") provides a cloud security posture management service. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. Contact: privacy@aegis.example.

2. Data we process

  • Account data: name, work email, organisation, role.
  • Cloud connection metadata: role ARNs, project/subscription IDs, and encrypted credentials used solely to run read-only audits.
  • Scan results: resource identifiers and misconfiguration findings from your cloud accounts.
  • Usage & logs: request metadata, IP, timestamps for security and reliability.
  • Billing: handled by our payment processor (Stripe); we do not store card numbers.

3. How we use it

To provide and secure the service, generate findings and compliance reports, send alerts you configure, process payments, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data.

4. Cloud credentials

Credentials are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and used only to perform read-only API calls. We never modify your resources and never expose secrets in the UI.

5. Sub-processors

We use infrastructure and service providers (e.g., cloud hosting, Stripe for payments, email/alert delivery). A current list is available on request.

6. Retention

Findings and audit logs are retained per your plan/configuration and then purged. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Your rights

Subject to law, you may access, correct, export, or delete your data. Email privacy@aegis.example.

8. Security

See our Security page. No system is 100% secure, but we apply industry-standard safeguards.

9. Changes

We'll post updates here and, for material changes, notify you.
